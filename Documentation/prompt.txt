Elevator Pitch:
EverRealm: Exiles is a medieval extraction game being developed in Unity, serving as a focused spin-off of the larger EverRealm vision. It draws inspiration from Minecraft and Cube World, distilling their core strengths into a tighter, session-based format. From Minecraft, it inherits procedurally generated voxel maps filled with lootable resources, hidden caches, and destructible environments. From Cube World, it takes fast, fluid combat featuring dodging, diverse weapons, and dynamic encounters.

Rather than an open-world sandbox, Exiles drops players into randomly generated maps with one goal: gather as much loot as possible and extract before time runs out or other threats close in. Each run presents a different layout with scattered resources, dungeons, enemy encampments, and extraction points that force players to make constant risk-versus-reward decisions.

Progression between runs allows players to upgrade their loadout, unlock new weapon types, and build out their character. The core gameplay loop — drop in, fight, loot, extract — is designed to be replayable and mechanically tight while also serving as a proving ground for the combat and procedural generation systems that will eventually feed into the full EverRealm experience.
